This page focuses only on the new Breakout mission update and its supporting systems: new player troops, new buildings, new enemies, new combat UI, stratagems, warships and optimisation work.

New Player Tools

The update expands the player's tactical options by adding support-focused troops and stronger defensive infrastructure. The goal is to make fort defence more active than simply surviving waves.

Sharpshooters

New player troops focused on ranged fire support. They give the player another way to control enemy pressure before it reaches the fort line.

Medics

New support troops designed around keeping the player, party and allied troops alive during extended defensive fights.

Reloading Combat Loop

Magazine size, reloading mechanics and matching UI were added so ranged combat has clearer pacing and feedback.

New Player Buildings

Breakout adds new player-side structures that support both the economy and the defensive mission flow.

Hospitals

Hospitals heal and buff nearby player units, party members and troops, giving the stronghold a support hub during defence.

Forts, Walls and Gates

Fort structures create the core defensive space for the Breakout mission, with walls and gates supporting a stronger hold-the-line setup.

Mills

Mills generate food for the player, adding another economy layer to support troops and defensive preparation.

Enemy Expansion

The enemy roster is expanded to make the fort defence scenario more varied and dangerous.

New Melee Mech Types

  • Two new enemy melee mech types added to increase close-range pressure.
  • Designed to threaten defensive lines and force the player to react instead of only relying on ranged control.

Enemy Warships

  • Enemy warships add a larger battlefield threat to the mission.
  • Their presence helps the update feel bigger than a standard ground-only defence encounter.

Stratagem System

A new stratagem system gives the player access to orbital strike support. This adds a deliberate, high-impact layer on top of troop control, base defence and direct combat.

Orbital Strike

One of two new orbital strike tools added through the stratagem system for dealing with powerful enemies.

Orbital Barrage

An orbital barrage to lay down fire over a large area to respond to clustered or dangerous enemy pushes.

Breakout: Fort Defence

Breakout is the new mission built around defending a fort or stronghold. The added troops, buildings, enemies, warships and orbital strikes all support this mission direction.

  • Hold the stronghold against enemy attacks.
  • Use forts, walls and gates to define the defensive front.
  • Use Hospitals to keep nearby player forces alive and buffed.
  • Use Mills to generate food and support the player's economy.
  • Call in orbital stratagems when the defensive line is under heavy pressure.

Optimisation & Polish

Alongside the new content, the update includes major optimisation work focused on the areas most likely to become expensive during a large defence mission.

Enemy Optimisations

Improved enemy performance to better support larger battles and heavier pressure during the Breakout mission.

Settler Optimisations

Player settler behaviour was a major focus so the economy layer can scale more cleanly during active defence gameplay.

Combat UI Polish

Magazine size, reload mechanics and UI feedback were added to make weapon state easier to read in combat.

For this mission update, my work focused on expanding the player-side army and buildings, adding enemy threats, implementing the stratagem layer, building the Breakout mission structure, and improving performance for larger battles.

  • Implemented new gameplay systems and mission content in Unity/C#.
  • Added player buildings: Hospitals, Forts, Walls, Gates and Mills.
  • Added new player troops: Sharpshooters and Medics.
  • Added two new enemy melee mech types and enemy warships.
  • Added a stratagem system with two orbital strikes.
  • Optimised enemies and player settlers for larger mission scenarios.
  • Added magazine size, reloading mechanics and combat UI feedback.
