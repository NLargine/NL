Starfall Legacy

A co-op anime action roguelite in Unreal Engine 5, C++ and the Gameplay Ability System. Work in progress.

View Project

Overview

Starfall Legacy is a solo project I'm building in Unreal Engine 5. You play Annie, a witch who casts with her hands, alongside Alice, a support mage. A second player can take Alice in online co-op; otherwise she fights beside you as an AI companion. Runs move through rooms of enemy waves, elites and a boss, and the cards you pick between rooms build your spells. Between runs you return to a camp hub to upgrade, change your loadout and talk to the party.

All my earlier projects are Unity and C#. Starfall Legacy is my move to Unreal and C++: networked gameplay built on GAS from the start, a toon look, and editor tooling that generates the AI trees and animation graphs from code.

Status: playable prototype. The full run loop works end to end in co-op (hub → rooms → elite → boss → back to camp).

What's Built So Far

Combat on GAS

Light and heavy attacks, a tap-to-dash and hold-to-Blink dodge, and four equipped skills on two swappable bars, all as Gameplay Abilities with client prediction so they feel responsive in co-op. Hits run through one damage path with local hit-stop, knockback via root motion and poise breaks.

Fire, ice and lightning stack statuses that trigger reactions such as Overload, Shatter and Spread. Elite enemies carry an element shield that only its weakness breaks quickly.

Hub Camp

The camp between runs has a portal with a co-op ready check, a shrine for permanent upgrades, a loadout board, a training dummy with a DPS readout and a campfire for visual-novel style conversations written in Ink.

Systems Overview

  • Roguelite run director: rooms, waves, elites, rewards, door votes, XP and run summary
  • Card rewards: blessings that hook into damage, statuses and mana, plus skill rank-ups
  • Party of two with swapping, and a StateTree companion AI that heals, wards and follows orders
  • Enemy roster with telegraphed attacks, and a two-phase golem boss with breakable armour
  • Lock-on camera, soft aim and strafing locomotion driven by motion matching
  • Data-table balance that writes every tuning number into class defaults at startup
  • Substrate toon shading and an outline post-process for the anime look
