Overview

Ghost Protocol is an in-development project centered on a spy-action fantasy with a stronger emphasis on ranged combat, character presentation, and tone than the more systems-heavy projects elsewhere in the portfolio.

At this stage, the page functions more as a direction snapshot than a full case study: it shows visual intent, early combat framing, and the kind of atmosphere the project is aiming for.

Current Focus

The main interest in Ghost Protocol is shaping a clean action identity: readable weapon use, stylish presentation, and character-led visual tone. It sits in the portfolio as an active work in progress rather than a finished systems showcase.
