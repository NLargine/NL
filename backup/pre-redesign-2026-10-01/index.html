01 / What I build

Games as
connected systems.

My work sits where design and engineering meet. I build the playable layer, the data underneath it and the production tools needed to keep a large project understandable. Iron Remnant is the clearest expression of that approach.

Player-facing systems

Combat, abilities, progression, equipment, troop control and interfaces designed as one readable experience.

Simulation & AI

Economies, construction, settlements and adaptive opposition that create objectives instead of scripted spectacle alone.

Production tooling

Custom Unity tools for inspecting state, validating content and balancing interconnected data safely at scale.
