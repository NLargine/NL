About

My work sits where design and engineering meet: the playable layer, the data underneath it and the tools that keep a large project understandable.

  • Player-facing systemsCombat, abilities, progression, equipment, troop control and interfaces designed as one readable experience.
  • Simulation & AIEconomies, construction, settlements and adaptive enemies that create objectives, not just scripted moments.
  • Production toolingCustom Unity tools for inspecting state, validating content and balancing connected data safely.
