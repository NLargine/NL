This page focuses on the Counter Attack mission update: a bigger offensive scenario with subobjectives, new player and enemy production, revamped enemy forces, troop leveling, airstrikes and improved enemy decision-making.

Offensive Mission Structure

Counter Attack pushes Iron Remnant into a larger offensive mission format. Instead of only surviving pressure, the player advances through enemy-held territory, breaks production points and completes layered subobjectives before the final push.

  • Built around a bigger mission map with multiple offensive lanes and objective pressure points.
  • Subobjectives give the player smaller targets to clear.
  • The player HQ remains strategically important, giving the AI a meaningful target while the player commits to the counter-attack.
  • Vehicle troops, artillery and stratagems are designed to support base assault rather than only defensive survival.

New Player Tools

The player's army expands with heavier offensive options, including the first vehicle-focused troop additions and a new building for producing artillery units.

Mobile Artillery

A new troop vehicle focused on long-range bombardment. It gives the player a way to pressure enemy bases, clustered troops and fortified positions from safer distances.

Tank

A new armoured troop vehicle for frontline assaults, designed to anchor offensive pushes and absorb pressure while infantry and support fire advance behind it.

Artillery Foundry

A new player building that supports the production and progression of heavier artillery-focused troops for the Counter Attack mission flow.

Enemy Expansion

The enemy side has been expanded with new production buildings and a revamped combat roster, making offensive pushes more contested and less predictable.

Enemy Production

  • Enemy Mech Barracks added to train mech-based threats from dedicated production points.
  • Enemy Artillery Foundry added so the opposing base can field heavier long-range pressure.
  • Enemy AI can choose which barracks should train units based on objective proximity and battlefield need.

Revamped Enemy Forces

  • Enemy transports added to move pressure across the battlefield.
  • Mech lasers and Mech machine gunners added for new ranged threat profiles.
  • New Enemy Skirmisher type to counter the player and sharpshooters.
  • Gunships revamped and added as stronger airborne threats during the assault.

Stratagems & Airstrikes

Counter Attack expands the stratagem layer with four new options: one new orbital strike and three new airstrikes under a new ability type. The aim is to give the player more decisive offensive tools during large assaults.

New Orbital Stratagem

A new orbital option adds another high-impact support call-in for clearing dangerous targets or opening a path through enemy defences.

Airstrike Ability Type

A new stratagem category built around aircraft-delivered attacks, expanding support beyond orbital-only abilities.

Three New Airstrikes

Three airstrike variants add more choice in how the player handles entrenched enemies, spread-out forces and high-priority threats.

Troop & Enemy Leveling

The mission update adds leveling support for player troops and enemies, helping larger battles scale more clearly across the mission and giving unit progression a stronger role in combat balance.

Player Troop Leveling

Player troops can scale through levels, allowing the player to fight masses of enemies with stronger troops.

Enemy Leveling

Enemies can also scale by level, allowing the mission to introduce stronger threats as the player pushes deeper into enemy territory.

Mission Scaling

Leveling supports a bigger mission arc, where late-stage fights can escalate without relying only on larger unit counts.

Improved Enemy AI

The enemy AI has been upgraded to make smarter production and spawning decisions during offensive missions. Instead of acting from a single static pattern, it can evaluate objectives, barracks placement and current threats.

  • Chooses which barracks or production building should train the next wave.
  • Trains troops closer to the AI objective or the player HQ when that produces stronger pressure.
  • Uses threat weighting to decide where enemy pressure should be applied.
  • Supports different troop types across mech barracks, artillery foundries and revamped enemy forces.
  • Creates a more reactive enemy during large offense-focused missions with multiple subobjectives.
